Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing marketing campaign performance by transforming raw campaign data into meaningful business insights. The dashboard provides an interactive view of campaign effectiveness and helps identify which campaigns generate better engagement, conversions, revenue, and return on investment.

Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks important marketing performance indicators such as:

Impressions – Overall campaign reach
Clicks – User engagement
Conversions – Successful campaign outcomes
Revenue – Revenue generated from campaigns
Spend – Marketing expenditure
ROI – Return generated relative to marketing investment
Business Objective

The objective of this project is to help marketing teams evaluate campaign effectiveness, identify high-performing campaigns, understand customer engagement, and make data-driven decisions regarding marketing budget allocation and campaign optimization.
